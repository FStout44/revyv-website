REVYV(re·vive)/rəˈvīv/recharge. restore. repeat.

REVYV is a custom-built recovery room inside your gym. Sauna, cold plunge, compression and red light therapy share one quiet room, a few steps from where you train.

The Room

Recover like a pro, without leaving your gym.

01

Sauna

A traditional hot-rock sauna with tiered benches and room for your training partners. Pour the water, take the heat and let the session settle.

02

Cold Plunge

A wood-wrapped plunge, kept cold and filtered. Rinse off, step in and stay as long as you can hold it.

Also in the room
03

Compression

Compression boots for tired legs. Sit back, zip in and let them work while you rest.

04

Red Light

Red light therapy for a quiet few minutes at the end of your session, before you head back out.

Rep. REVYV. Repeat.

Train. Recover. Do it again.

How It Works

Recovery, built into the place you train.

Inside your gym

Every REVYV room is designed and built into space the gym already has, so recovery is part of your session and never a second trip.

One monthly membership

Members pay monthly and use the room as often as they like, before class, after class or on a rest day.

Finished like a spa

Dark walls, warm wood, soft light and fresh towels. Each room is custom-built to feel like somewhere you want to stay.
